# Individual Contribution

I led the threshold optimization and advocated for selecting 0.40 as the operational cutoff to balance recall and precision while respecting retention capacity constraints. I also contributed to separating segmentation logic from churn prediction to ensure interpretability and avoid leakage.

One key decision I supported was prioritizing ROC-AUC as the primary evaluation metric because churn is fundamentally a ranking problem. This allowed the model to be aligned with business prioritization rather than simple classification accuracy.

From this project, I learned the importance of translating predictive performance into operationally realistic decisions, particularly when intervention resources are limited.
